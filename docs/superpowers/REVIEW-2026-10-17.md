## Medium

### ✅ fixed (batch 1) M1 — An open pen draft keeps the matrices it took at resume *(area 6; area 3 suspicion)*
`src/tools/pen.ts:85-86` (`toDoc`/`toLocal` through `d.world`/`d.inv`, captured at ~378-403). The
pen has no `settle` hook, so store edits that call `cancelActiveGesture()` (flip, nudge, resize,
Document settings' Apply) leave the draft alone; only `discard` (replace/undo/redo) drops it.
Scenario (reproduced): path with `translate(100,0)`, resume from its end, ⇧H flips it, click
(60,30), Enter → the node lands at (180,30). A nudge offsets every later click; Scale drawing or
Crop/extend leaves a draft — even a new path's — in the old page coordinates; the overlay draws the
draft through the stale matrix too. **Fix:** give the pen a `settle` that recomputes `world`/`inv`
from the path's current state (or finishes the draft like Enter); at least have `applyDocumentSize`
settle tool drafts.

### ✅ fixed (batch 1) M2 — A pill-shaped rect (rx = half its short side) often reopens as a plain path *(area 5)*
`src/svg/parse.ts:481-489`: rx and ry are clamped separately and compared with `===`. After a
resize or Scale drawing `h` is an arbitrary float and `rx = h/2`; `fmt` rounds rx and h separately,
so `fmt(rx)` can exceed `fmt(h)/2`, ry is clamped below it and the rect becomes `pathNode(...)` —
own-format file, nothing reported, handle kept, corner control gone. Reproduced: 263 of 1,000
random Scale drawings of a 100×20 rx 10 rect. **Fix:** treat `|rx − ry| ≤ 1e-6` after clamping as
equal (keep `min`), or have the writer clamp the rounded radius to `fmt(min(w,h))/2`.

### ✅ fixed (batches 1, 2) M3 — More properties that change the picture are ignored unreported *(area 5; same class as H2)*
`src/svg/parse.ts:105-113` (`UNREPRESENTABLE`) lacks `stroke-miterlimit` (Illustrator writes 10 on
nearly every stroke; ours is 4, so joins between ~11.5° and ~29° bevel), `paint-order`,
`vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"` and `mix-blend-mode`. Reproduced: `dropped: []`, picture
changed; an own file edited elsewhere keeps its handle and ⌘S writes the property away
(invariant 10). **Fix:** add them with their plain values (`4`, `normal`, `none`, `normal`); report
miterlimit only when the join is miter (or write `stroke-miterlimit` ourselves), to avoid noise.

### ✅ fixed (batch 1) M4 — Autosave never reconnects after a lost IndexedDB connection *(area 7; 09-30 suspicion proven)*
`src/persist/autosave.ts:52-72`: `dbPromise` is cleared only on open failure, `onclose` and
`onversionchange`. Safari can drop the connection without `onclose`; every later
`db.transaction` throws, `reported` latches after one notice, and autosave is silently dead for the
session — then iPadOS reclaims the backgrounded tab. Reproduced with a fake `indexedDB` (opens
stayed at 1). Same pattern in `src/persist/font-cache.ts:77-104`. **Fix:** on a transaction
failure close the db and clear `dbPromise` before rethrowing; reset `reported` after a successful
write so a later failure is announced again.

### ✅ fixed (batch 1) M5 — The Google Fonts face cache stores bytes before they parse: one bad 200 breaks that face for good *(areas 2, 4, 7 ×3; 09-30 suspicion proven)*
`src/state/appState.svelte.ts:2200` (`faceLoader` calls `putFace` before `font.ts:188` parses).
A captive portal or filtering proxy's HTML with status 200 is cached in IndexedDB; every later
preview/Add/reshape at that weight fails ("couldn't be read", or "Reload the page" — which doesn't
help), across sessions, with no way to clear it. Reproduced with `memoryFontStore`. **Fix:** cache
only after a successful parse, or delete the key and refetch once when cached bytes fail to parse.

### ✅ fixed (batch 2) M6 — On a touch screen 240px can't be kept as the sidebar width *(areas 1, 7, 8 ×3)*
`src/persist/preferences.ts:97,150-153`: the "stored 240 means not chosen" migration (`notDefault`)
runs in `sanitizePrefs`, which `setPrefs` runs on **every write**
(`appState.svelte.ts:792`). A drag released at 240 snaps to 280 on release; arrow-stepping the grip
narrower cycles 248 → 280 → 272 … and never goes below 248. Reproduced. **Fix:** apply the migration
once when loading (behind a prefs version), never on write.

### ✅ fixed (batch 2) M7 — A tap on the sidebar grip saves a width the user never chose *(areas 7, 8 ×2)*
`src/lib/Sidebar.svelte:92-115` (`gripDown` sets `liveWidth = widthPx`, `gripUp` commits it). In
portrait the shown width is clamped to half the viewport; a tap with no movement saves that clamped
value, so landscape keeps it for good — review M18's symptom back through a tap. With `sidebarPx:
null` on touch, a stray tap saves 280 as a choice. `gripKey` steps from the clamped width too.
**Fix:** commit only if the pointer moved; step keys from the stored width.

## Low

### Undo & brackets
- ✅ fixed (batch 2) **L1 — Undo/redo during a UI bracket splits the rest of the drag** *(areas 1, 3 ×2; 09-30
  suspicion proven)*. `session.ts:36-46` `undoSession`/`redoSession` call `endGesture`; the
  NumberField/colour/Midpoint drag keeps committing without a bracket, one undo step per value, and
  a mid-drag redo wipes the redo stack. Reproduced on the real store. Related: two quick TextPanel
  drags can share/close each other's bracket (`finishCharDrag` + `beginDocGesture` reusing the
  epoch; traced, needs a pending outline — practically unreachable with a loaded face). **Fix:**
  ignore undo/redo while a UI bracket is open; bump the epoch on every `beginUiGesture`.
- ✅ fixed (batch 2) **L2 — A Properties-panel drag during a canvas text session closes the typing bracket** *(area
  3)*. `PropertiesPanel.svelte` passes bare `endDocGesture` as `onliveend`; TextPanel's
  `finishCharDrag` guards `insideTyping()`, this doesn't. One session becomes two undo steps
  (reproduced on the store; whether iPad keeps the textarea focused during the drag is unchecked).
  **Fix:** an `endUiGesture` that skips the close while `insideTyping()`.
- ✅ fixed (batch 2) **L3 — Undo while a brush stroke is still landing undoes the previous edit, then the stroke wipes
  redo** *(area 4)*. `appState.svelte.ts:513-519`, `:1790`. Needs the first Paper load. Not old L6
  (that is a stroke joining a bracket). Reproduced. **Fix:** await `brushChain` in undo/redo.

- ✅ fixed (batch 3) **L5 — Open and New ignore a brush stroke still landing** *(area 4)*. `project-io.ts:25-27`,
  `NewDocumentDialog.svelte:71` check only `app.dirty`; reproduced. **Fix:** count
  `app.brushPending.length > 0` as unsaved; re-check after the picker resolves.
- ✅ fixed (batch 3) **L6 — A connection dropped mid-download gives the wrong message** *(area 4)*.
  `google-fonts.ts:136,155`: `res.text()`/`res.arrayBuffer()` sit outside the try that maps to
  `FontDownloadError`, so it reads "couldn't be read" or "Reload the page". **Fix:** wrap both.
- ✅ fixed (batch 3) **L7 — A METADATA.pb that parses to nothing is cached for the session** *(area 2)*.
  `appState.svelte.ts:2213`: a portal's 200 resolves to `[]`, not a rejection. Reproduced. **Fix:**
  throw a `FontDownloadError` on an empty face list.

### Input & UI
- ✅ fixed (batch 3) **L8 — A layer-row drag ignores document changes** *(area 1)*. `LayersPanel.svelte` ~169-281: no
  `onlostpointercapture` on the grips and no gesture-cancel hook; ⌫/⌘Z removing the dragged row
  leaves a floating ghost, a global `grabbing` cursor and an endless edge-scroll loop until Escape;
  a row inserted mid-drag shifts the drop by one; a second pointer overwrites `dragging`. Traced.
  **Fix:** `onlostpointercapture` → `endDrag(e, false)`; ignore `startDrag` while dragging; finish
  the drag when `app.doc` changes.
- ⛔ declined (batch 3, by design) **L9 — Letters in a focused `<select>` switch tools** *(area 8)*. `keys.ts:51-57` treats a select

  selection. **Fix:** let a select keep plain printable keys, Enter, Escape and Backspace. *Declined:*
  invariant 50 deliberately lets tool keys through a select, so V or B still work after a mouse
  pick — the commoner case in this app; type-ahead would cost that.
- ✅ fixed (batch 3) **L10 — TextPanel's Download row appears asynchronously and pushes the rows below it** *(areas 2,
  8 ×2; 09-30 suspicion proven; invariant 23)*. `TextPanel.svelte:61-72,318-329`. **Fix:** reserve
  the row at once as an `aria-disabled` "Looking up…" placeholder.
- ✅ fixed (batch 3) **L11 — Layer collapse state in the drawer is lost on every close** *(area 8)*. Two `Sidebar`
  instances, `collapsed` per instance (`LayersPanel.svelte:44`). **Fix:** move it into the store.
- ✅ fixed (batch 3) **L12 — Scale drawing with an empty W/H says "Too small"** *(area 8)*.
  `DocumentSettingsDialog.svelte:33`: `Number(null)` is 0. **Fix:** NaN when the side is null.

## Unverified suspicions (not proven; worth a look when nearby)
- **Text:** the reused textarea keeps its native undo history when re-entered with the same text
  (`TextEditField.svelte:118` skips assigning an equal value) — whether WebKit then lets ⌘Z restore
  another title's typing needs a device; placing + typing is two undo steps (confirmed, a design
  question). Opening Document settings (or any top-bar edit) during a text session may commit inside
  the typing bracket if iOS doesn't blur the textarea on the tap.
- **Async:** a Safari IndexedDB that stops answering (not erroring) would hang `faceLoader`, leaving
  `titleRunning` set and every title edit queued forever; `deleteCurrentLayer` counts children
  before its confirm, so a stroke landing meanwhile is deleted uncounted; `autosaveRecord` excludes
  `brushPending`; font files load from `@main` and the cached METADATA is never refreshed.
- **Persistence:** a `pagehide` write may not land on iOS; the manifest has no 192/512 PNG icons;
  `test:ipad`'s save check stubs `canShare`, so the download fallback is never exercised.
- **Geometry:** Scale drawing at an absurd k (1e-7) rounds tiny shapes to 0 on save; turning a
  stroke on after Scale drawing shows the unscaled width (`scaleDetails` skips `stroke === null` —
  maybe intended); `invert`'s absolute 1e-12 threshold makes a `scale(1e-6)` node silently
  unresizable; undoing a Scale drawing doesn't bring back `gradientMemory` (likely accepted).
- **Files:** non-ASCII element names fail the whole open with `XmlError` (reported, not silent);
  a node pasted out of a group with opacity loses the group's opacity; an open subpath's first `in`
  and last `out` handles are never written.
- **Input & UI:** a dock Shift *held* with a finger counts as latched, so a miss clears (matches the
  code's comment; invariant 38 wants a ruling); long press in Fingers-select pan doesn't restore the
  ≤10px pan; Document settings' AnchorGrid and refusal line appear/disappear and move Apply
  (spec-sanctioned, conflicts with invariant 23); Select ▸ Invert has no reason title when nothing
  is in reach; two mounted Sidebars render everything twice below 900px (performance group).
